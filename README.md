# Johto & Kanto Merged Encounters

### A Wild Encounter Expansion for Pokémon Gen 2 Recomp

**Version:** 2.0.0

This mod seamlessly merges the version-exclusive wild encounters of Pokémon Gold and Silver into a single game file, while also adding the Kanto/Johto Starters, Fossils, Trade Evolutions, and Legendary/Mythical Pokémon as rare natural encounters. 

**IMPORTANT NOTE:** This mod strictly focuses on wild grass, cave, and surfing encounters. **It does NOT include:**
* **Swarm Pokémon** (Qwilfish and Remoraid remain tied to their vanilla phone call swarm mechanics).

---

## 1. Version Exclusives Merged

Version-exclusive Pokémon from both Gold and Silver now coexist in their natural habitats. Encounter percentages have been mathematically balanced within the engine's strict 7-slot limitation to preserve the vanilla ecosystem.

### Johto Restored Encounters
* **Route 30 & 31:** Ledyba & Spinarak / Caterpie & Weedle lines
* **Route 32 & 33:** Ekans
* **Route 36 & 37:** Growlithe & Vulpix
* **Route 38 & 39:** Meowth
* **Route 42:** Mankey
* **Route 45:** Gligar & Skarmory / Teddiursa & Phanpy
* **Union Cave:** Sandshrew
* **Ice Path:** Delibird
* **Mt. Silver (Outside & Inside):** Donphan & Ursaring
* **Route 41 (Surf):** Mantine

### Kanto Restored Encounters
* **Mt. Moon:** Sandshrew & Sandslash
* **Routes 3, 4, 26 & 27:** Ekans & Arbok
* **Routes 5, 6, 7 & 8:** Meowth & Persian
* **Route 9:** Mankey & Primeape

## Installation

Download `catch_em_all_gen2-2.0.0.zip` from the [Releases](https://github.com/zingamau-dev/catch_em_all_gen2/releases) page, then in the game:

**Launcher → MODS → Import mod .zip**, or in a running game
**START → MODS → Import mod .zip**.

Because this mod includes its GitHub repository in the manifest, the launcher will keep it up to date automatically. When a new version is published, you can install it directly from the launcher's **Find mods** tab without having to manually download files again.
